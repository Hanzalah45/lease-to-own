What shipped

From application to payment schedule

Milestone 2 (lease & ownership pricing) and Milestone 3 (risk assessment) landed together, because the pricing engine needed a real application to price and the risk engine needed a real applicant to score.

Backend — pricing & risk

  • Early Purchase Option (EPO) formula First 90 days: cash price minus rent paid. After that: cash price minus half of rent paid, plus remaining term value. Reaches $0 at full term — ownership transfers automatically.
  • Automatic payment schedule One row per month of the term, generated the moment an application is approved. Paid-count is tracked live and flips the lease to "owned" at term end.
  • Risk scoring on submission Checks identity, employment, and bank data on file; a 100-point score comes back with each leg marked verified or pending. Income under 3× the monthly payment is flagged for review.
  • Apartment residences auto-decline Per the underwriting rule, an application is declined automatically before scoring if the residence type is an apartment.
  • Plaid bank connection Customer links a bank account; the platform stores the connection and marks bank verification complete.

Frontend — review & workflow

  • Full application wizard, both sides Equipment, lease terms, customer info, and risk/verification steps — usable by admins on a customer's behalf or by the customer directly.
  • Admin review console A status pipeline (submitted → under review → needs info → approved → completed → processed → funded) with an action banner and editable customer, lease, equipment, and risk cards.
  • In-app notifications Admins are notified when an application comes in; customers are notified when its status changes or their bank verifies.
  • Bank-connect widget Customer-side Plaid Link button showing connected accounts and verification state.

Verified, not just built

  1. Customer completes the application wizard and it lands in the admin queue with a risk score already attached.
  2. Admin reviews, edits terms if needed, and approves — the full payment schedule generates automatically.
  3. EPO figure recalculates correctly at each stage of the term, month by month.
  4. An apartment-residence application is declined automatically, before it reaches a reviewer.

Worth knowing before Milestone 4

What "verified" means right now

Nothing below is broken — it's the honest scope of what's wired up today, flagged so nobody assumes more coverage than exists yet.

Bank verification is connection-level. Plaid confirms an account is linked and returns basic account info. It does not yet pull deposit history or pay frequency — that's a further Plaid call, not built yet.
Identity and employment are self-reported. "Verified" means the customer's information is on file, not that it's been checked against a government ID service or an employer. No third-party identity or credit-bureau check is wired up.
Risk red flags aren't generated yet. The red-flag list is in the schema and on the admin screen, but nothing currently writes a flag to it — it will always show empty until that logic is added.
Payments are recorded manually. Admins mark a payment paid/failed/refunded by hand. No live payment processor (Stripe, autopay charge, etc.) is connected yet.
Contract generation is still pending. Approving an application generates the payment schedule, not a signed contract — that's Milestone 5.
